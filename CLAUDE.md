# NFL Hub

One reliable NFL app (season odds, live games, player analytics) replacing Dan's earlier one-shot NFL projects.

- **Start here:** `docs/PLAN.md` is the agreed design, the milestones and who builds what. Read it fully before changing anything.
- **Then read `docs/CONTEXT.md`:** why the plan says what it says, Dan's own words, what was verified and what was only assumed. The plan is a strong default, not gospel: think for yourself, and if something looks wrong or could be simpler, raise it with Dan and update the plan.
- **Status (2026-11-16):** M0 (foundation) is built, live and QA-fixed (`docs/qa/2026-11-13-M0.md`; QA-M0-14 is Dan's call). Five sources (ESPN FPI, DraftKings, Polymarket, Kalshi, Bovada) are captured twice daily by `.github/workflows/daily.yml`; the nflverse stage downloads and checks play-by-play, player stats, games and players; the site shows a bare status page. M1-1 to M1-4 are done: the hub model runs daily as the `model` job (a forecast after every game day since 2020, `data/model/{season}.json`), v3's real 2026 snapshots are imported (2026-09-08 to 2026-11-10, marked `imported`), and the `compile` job writes chart-ready `data/season/{season}/series.json` and `weekly.json` with consensus. M1-5 is done: the design system (`app/src/ui/`, preferences in `app/src/lib/prefs.ts`, everything on one page at `#/styleguide`; plan section 11 says how to use it), not yet reviewed by Dan. M1-6 is done: the chart, source tabs and view-state model are in `app/src/ui/chart/` (plan 8.2 says how they fit), the Season page shows two real charts as the exemplar, and a Playwright smoke suite (`e2e/`, `npm run e2e`, also in CI) proves at 360 and 1280 px that switching source keeps the view. M1-9 is done: the status page at `#/status` uses the design system and stale markers follow plan 7.4. M1-7 and M1-8 are done: Season has Outlook (every team metric, 2020 to 2026), Standings (records and seeds if the season ended today, with hub odds), Leaders (`pipeline/leaders.ts`, daily, 2020 on) and Awards (2026), with a season selector. M1 is built and waits for its QA pass (`docs/QA.md`) and Dan's M1 checkpoint. M2 (Live) was started early with Dan's approval, since it does not touch the Season code: M2-1 (ESPN live client, `app/src/lib/espn/`, `docs/SOURCES.md` "ESPN live games"), M2-4 (replay harness, `#/live?replay=401873063&speed=20`, plan 9.5) and M2-2 (spoiler delay, plan 9.3: views cut by `wallclock` instead of a payload buffer) are done, with bare Live pages that M2-5 and M2-6 replace. Live pages must draw only what `useDelayed()` returns. The schemas were checked against the last hour of a real game in progress (2026-11-16, `docs/SOURCES.md`): no surprises, but ESPN's plays arrive a median of 43 s after they happen, so the page can only match a TV that is at least that far behind. Next for Fable: M2-3 (EP table and EPA estimator). Next for Opus: M2-5 to M2-8 after M2-3. Dan's M0 checkpoint (three days of unattended captures) runs to 11-17. Run `npm run check` before every commit.
