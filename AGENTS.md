# NFL Hub

One reliable NFL app (season odds, live games, player analytics) replacing Dan's earlier one-shot NFL projects.

- **Start here:** `docs/PLAN.md` is the agreed design, the milestones and who builds what. Read it fully before changing anything.
- **Then read `docs/CONTEXT.md`:** why the plan says what it says, Dan's own words, what was verified and what was only assumed. The plan is a strong default, not gospel: think for yourself, and if something looks wrong or could be simpler, raise it with Dan and update the plan.
- **Status (2026-11-15):** M0 (foundation) is built, live and QA-fixed (`docs/qa/2026-11-13-M0.md`; QA-M0-14 is Dan's call). Five sources (ESPN FPI, DraftKings, Polymarket, Kalshi, Bovada) are captured twice daily by `.github/workflows/daily.yml`; the nflverse stage downloads and checks play-by-play, player stats, games and players; the site shows a bare status page. M1-1 to M1-4 are done: the hub model runs daily as the `model` job (a forecast after every game day since 2020, `data/model/{season}.json`), v3's real 2026 snapshots are imported (2026-09-08 to 2026-11-10, marked `imported`), and the `compile` job writes chart-ready `data/season/{season}/series.json` and `weekly.json` with consensus. M1-5 is done: the design system (`app/src/ui/`, preferences in `app/src/lib/prefs.ts`, everything on one page at `#/styleguide`; plan section 11 says how to use it), not yet reviewed by Dan. M1-6 is done: the chart, source tabs and view-state model are in `app/src/ui/chart/` (plan 8.2 says how they fit), the Season page shows two real charts as the exemplar, and a Playwright smoke suite (`e2e/`, `npm run e2e`, also in CI) proves at 360 and 1280 px that switching source keeps the view. M1-9 is done: the status page at `#/status` uses the design system and stale markers follow plan 7.4. The Season Outlook and Awards pages (most of M1-7) show every team metric for 2020 to 2026 and every award for 2026, with a season selector. Next: Standings (the rest of M1-7); its task row in the plan holds hand-over notes. Dan's M0 checkpoint (three days of unattended captures) runs to 11-17. Run `npm run check` before every commit.
